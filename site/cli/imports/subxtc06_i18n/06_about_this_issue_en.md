About this Issue
(c) SE Team 2024

Well here we are finally, Subliminal Extacy #6! And by 6, we mean over 6 weeks late for the official release after being submitted to the Wild Compo at Forever (and coming second)! After all, it wouldn't be a proper SE release if it wasn't delayed somehow, right? ;)

We've also taken on a new member to the SE Team, mikezt! Welcome to the team, we hope you enjoy your time with us and hopefully help things along so our releases aren't so delayed in future!

With this issue we're also distributing a demo version of a game titled Blumps written by mikezt! It's our first "cover tape" ever, and to top it off Mike also wrote an article for you to read about Blumps in this issue of SE.

We'd like to thank all those who sent in articles, music, gfx and ascii art.

And as always, don't forget that SE is only possible due to reader contributions. So if you want to see SE released more often, send in your articles! What are you waiting for? Support SE! Support the scene!
