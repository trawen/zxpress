3 SCREEN$ reimagined by Paulo Dias.

PAULO: I'm a novice in terms of pixel art and the program that I use is Multipaint, suggested by the very talented Andy Green. Trailblazer was my first real attempt in last October and my thoughts were trying to make a loading screen in a different perspective than the original, like if we were playing in a 3d environment. Match Point is responsible for having my Speccy as a Xmas '84 anticipated present; this was my tribute to the game, but since I didn't want to compete with the original screen I decided to draw a female player. Werewolves of London is based on Your Sinclair's cover art from November 87 and I was inspired after watching Werewolf of London, a really cool movie from the 30s.
