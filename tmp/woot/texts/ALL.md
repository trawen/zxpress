The focus on modern sprites make it more of a niche game, but classical tastes have already been catered for with Pixel Quest.

IIn a small village near Bremen, in 1846, Gunter Bikemayker stood forlorn in his workshop. IAt best I hoped for a fun curio I could ride about, and maybe use for short commutes during busy rail journeys. When the bike arrived, I was impressed by its style and compactness.
