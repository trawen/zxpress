Absorb resources and build, whilst avoiding the gaze of the titular sentinel. Genuinely scary when you're seen, and hurrying to teleport to safety. A masterpiece.

Despite the character based scrolling, and primitive looking UDG-style graphics there's a great game here - and a gameplay idea that I haven't seen replicated elsewhere often. Being forced to navigate around a maze whilst avoiding the ever moving boundary 'window'. It also shouts "Yippee!" when you complete a level and makes you bob yourself. So it'd be nice to have that 'talk' to me on my island.
