THE DARK - OLEG ORIGIN
MANY PEOPLE SAID THAT IT WAS NOT POSSIBLE TO CREATE A FIRST
PERSON SHOOTER FOR THE SPECTRUM (EVEN AFTER SEEING THE OLD
ATTEMPTS FROM THE RUSSIAN SCENE) FROM THE LIKES OF DOOM & QUAKE,BUT THE GREAT CODER OLEG ORIGIN DID IT AGAIN, AND PROGRAMMED A
MIRACULOUS PIECE OF SOFTWARE... IN ONLY 48K!
THE DARK IS A FPS WHERE YOU PLAY THE ROLE OF A HERO WHO RETURNS
TO HIS NATIVE LAND TO SET IT FREE FROM INVADING MONSTERS.
THE FEELING OF WALKING ALONE IN THE LABYRINTHS & BEING AMBUSHED AT ANY MOMENT IS, ALONG WITH THE REALISM AND SOLID ENGINE, THE
MAIN ATTRACTIVE POINT. THE GRAPHICS ARE WELL DRAWN THOUGH LACK
VARIETY. OTHER GOOD DETAILS ARE THE INTRO WITH SYNTHI-SPEECH ANDGOOD LEVEL DESIGN. THE LACK OF MUSIC PREVENTS IT BEING A 5/5, SOIT'S A SOLID 4/5.

CAR WARS - SALVAKANTERO
AUTHORED WITH THE LATEST TOOL FROM THE MOJON TWINS, THE MK2, CARWARS FEATURES A NEW POINT OF VIEW FOR THE CHURRERA STYLE
GAMES, AS WE ARE NOW A CAR AND THE CAMERA IS OVER US AS IN GAMESSUCH AS GAUNTLET OR RANARAMA. YOU HAVE TO COLLECT A NUMBER OF

AND A BALANCED CURVE OF DIFFICULTY.
